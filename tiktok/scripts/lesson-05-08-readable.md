## Lesson 5: Add E To ANT And ENT Words

English has hidden Spanish words all over it. Here is one pattern.

Words ending in ANT or ENT often become Spanish by adding E.

Important becomes importante.
Different becomes diferente.

And es means it is.

[on screen: it is important]
Say it in Spanish: it is important.
[beat]
Es importante. Good.

[on screen: it is different]
Say it in Spanish: it is different.
[beat]
Es diferente. Good.

Good. Now use the same rule on words I have not given you yet.

[on screen: constant]
Say constant in Spanish.
[beat]
Constante. Good.

[on screen: it is constant]
Now say it is constant in Spanish.
[beat]
Es constante. Good.

[on screen: excellent]
Say excellent in Spanish.
[beat]
Excelente. Good.

[on screen: it is evident]
Now say it is evident in Spanish.
[beat]
Es evidente. Good.

That is the payoff. One rule gave you constante, excelente, and evidente.

## Lesson 6: Mente Turns Words Into Adverbs

In English, words like normally and legally often end in LY.

Spanish has its own version of that ending: mente.

Normal becomes normalmente.

[on screen: normally]
Say normally in Spanish.
[beat]
Normalmente. Good.

[on screen: legally]
Say legally in Spanish.
[beat]
Legalmente. Good.

[on screen: naturally]
Say naturally in Spanish.
[beat]
Naturalmente. Good.

From the last pattern, constant became constante.

[on screen: constantly]
So say constantly in Spanish.
[beat]
Constantemente. Good.

Real is real in Spanish too.

[on screen: really]
One more: say really in Spanish.
[beat]
Realmente. Good.

Good. Same move each time. Take the word, attach mente.

## Lesson 7: The English J Sound Changes

Before we use words like general, we need one Spanish sound.

English has a J sound, like in jacket.

In many Spanish words, that sound becomes a soft breath in the throat.

[on screen: Argentina]
Escucha: Argentina.

Hear it in the middle? Not the English J from jacket. Let the air rub in the throat.

Now take the English word general. In English, it starts with that jacket sound.

[on screen: general]
Say the English word general in Spanish.
[beat]
En español: general. Good.

Good. The English J sound became the Spanish throat sound.

Now add mente. That gives us the English LY feeling.

[on screen: generally]
Say the English word generally in Spanish.
[beat]
En español: generalmente. Good.

Another one: the English word original has that same J sound in the middle.

[on screen: original]
Say the English word original in Spanish.
[beat]
En español: original. Good.

[on screen: originally]
Now say the English word originally in Spanish.
[beat]
En español: originalmente. Good.

Good. Listen for the sound, not just the letter.

Global keeps the hard G. General changes because English gives it that J sound.

## Lesson 8: ABLE And IBLE Words Carry Over

Here is another English ending that often carries into Spanish.

Words ending in IBLE or ABLE often work if you pronounce them the Spanish way.

Possible becomes posible.

[on screen: it is possible]
Say it is possible in Spanish.
[beat]
Es posible. Good.

Now use the same ending with probable.

[on screen: probable]
Say probable in Spanish.
[beat]
Probable. Good.

[on screen: it is probable]
Now say it is probable in Spanish.
[beat]
Es probable. Good.

[on screen: flexible]
Try one more word: say flexible in Spanish.
[beat]
Flexible. Good.

Now attach mente, the ending for many LY words.

[on screen: probably]
Say probably in Spanish.
[beat]
Probablemente. Good.

[on screen: possibly]
And say possibly in Spanish.
[beat]
Posiblemente. Good.

Good. Possible gave you posible, and then posiblemente.
