# TikTok Spanish Lessons 9-12 Readable Scripts

These are checkpoint lessons. Lesson 9 teaches `realmente` with positive
sentences. Lesson 10 teaches `normalmente` with `no es`. Lesson 11 rebuilds
`possible/impossible` with earlier tests. Lesson 12 adds `probablemente`.

Stage directions:

- `[on screen: ...]` means editor text.
- `[short beat]` means a quick silent prompt for recall.
- `[long beat]` means a longer pause for the viewer to build the full sentence.
- `[stretch beat]` means a final combo they should be able to get from earlier pieces.

## Lesson 9: Realmente Sentence Stack

This is why Spanish feels hackable.

Es means it is.

[on screen: it is]
[short beat]
En español: Es. Good.

The English word urgent changes in Spanish.

[on screen: urgent]
[short beat]
En español: urgente. Good.

Now use both pieces.

[on screen: it is urgent]
It is urgent.
[long beat]
En español: Es urgente. Good.

Important changes the same way.

[on screen: important]
[short beat]
En español: importante. Good.

[on screen: it is important]
It is important.
[long beat]
En español: Es importante. Good.

For many English words that end in L Y, Spanish uses mente.

Real becomes really.

En español: real. En español: realmente.

Now build it.

[on screen: really, it is urgent]
Really, it is urgent.
[long beat]
En español: Realmente es urgente. Good.

[on screen: really, it is important]
Really, it is important.
[long beat]
En español: Realmente es importante. Good.

Last one. You have not seen this full sentence here, but you have the pieces.

[on screen: really, it is not normal]
Really, it is not normal.
[stretch beat]
En español: Realmente no es normal. Good.

That is the point. You are building, not memorizing.
